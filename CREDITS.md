# Credits

Crimson Workbench for macOS is an independent port of
[ArasHueseyin/crimson-workbench](https://github.com/ArasHueseyin/crimson-workbench).
Thank you to Aras Hueseyin and the original project's contributors for the
open-source foundation.

The desktop application uses Tauri, React, TypeScript, Vite, Zustand, TanStack
Table/Virtual, Lucide, Vitest and Playwright. Their exact versions and licenses
are recorded in `Cargo.lock`, `app/package-lock.json`, and
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Selected archive, localization, table and save-codec code was adapted from
[bbfox0703/crimson-rs](https://github.com/bbfox0703/crimson-rs), commit
`b1b687bec8e38097dcb3a6dd39e5e96128d4f9c0`, under the MIT License. Its notices
are retained in `vendor/crimson-format/` and `licenses/`.

Crimson Desert and its contents belong to Pearl Abyss and their respective
owners. Game archives, tables, textures, saved games and third-party mods are
not included in this repository or its releases.
